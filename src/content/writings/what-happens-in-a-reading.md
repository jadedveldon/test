If you have never had a reading before, it is natural to wonder what it will be like. Here is the shape of it.

## Before

You send a short request with your name, your email and a few times that suit you. You do not need to explain your situation in the form. Sarvakali confirms a time, or suggests another, and sends a Google Meet link. Payment is by UPI, and an invoice is shared with you.

It helps to spend a few minutes before the call thinking about what you most want to understand. One clear question often opens more than ten small ones.

## During

The conversation usually begins simply: you say as much or as little as you like. Depending on the reading, Sarvakali may work with tarot or oracle cards, intuition, astrology or other tools, and will explain what they are seeing as they go. You can ask questions at any point.

## What a reading is, and is not

A reading is a mirror and a lantern: it can show you what is already present, light up choices, and help you understand a situation from a deeper place. It is not a replacement for medical, legal, financial or psychological advice, and no honest reader promises fixed outcomes. You always keep your own choice.

## After

Many people like to write down what came up while it is fresh. If something from the reading calls for further work, such as healing, a ritual or learning a practice, you can talk about that next.
