# Ideas for later

Ideas discussed with the site's owner and saved for later rounds. Each says what it is, why it matters, roughly what
it takes, and anything to be careful about. When one is built, move it to the change log and take it off this list.

Built so far from these talks: practice offered from every page, Today's line first on the homepage, today in the
moon card (2026-11-28), and instant pages (2026-11-28).

## Quick wins (a few hours each)

- **Today's line learns your tradition.** If a visitor keeps choosing, say, Buddhist texts in the library or keeps
  Jewish prayers, Today's line leans to that tradition on most days and still shows others now and then. Kept only in
  their browser. About three hours.
- **"Tomorrow" at the end of a practice.** The closing screen of a guided practice offers the next day ("Tomorrow is
  Sunday, the Sun's day: Greet the Sun") with one tap to add a gentle reminder to the visitor's own calendar. The
  calendar files already exist, on the tools page only. About half a day.
- **Care at night.** Between about 11 pm and 4 am (the visitor's time), the care note comes forward on the pages about
  grief, fear and healing: "If tonight is heavy, you don't have to carry it alone. Tele-MANAS 14416, free, any hour."
  Assumes nothing about the visitor. About two hours; pairs with the time of day below.
- **A "Still site" switch in the menu.** The calm version without the moving world exists already (for reduced-motion
  settings and in the prototype panel). A switch in the menu helps people sensitive to motion, on older phones or low
  battery, and anyone at prayer. About two hours.

## Bigger moves (half a day to a day each)

- **Share a mantra as a picture.** "Share as picture" on Today's line and each mantra page: a portrait image with
  the text in its script, transliteration and meaning, the day's colours, the mark and the link; on a phone it goes
  straight to WhatsApp. The biggest growth lever. Only texts open to all: nothing marked "learn with guidance" or
  "from Sarvakali's teaching", and no text for figures honoured without one. About a day.
- **"Together today."** The diya's shared count extended to Today's line and the guided practices: "Today, 214
  people said this line", "37 people sat with Kali today". Numbers only, nothing personal. Shown only above a
  threshold (about ten) so a new site never looks empty; best switched on once there are visitors. About half a day.
- **Pick up where you left off.** A guided practice left halfway is offered again on return: "Continue: Come before
  Kali, step 3 of 6". About half a day.
- **The site follows the visitor's time of day.** Dawn (a soft rose-gold), day, dusk (the lamp-lighting hour, the
  diya brought forward), night (deeper, the moon brighter). A gentle tint on the world's colours and a few words that
  change with the hour, all editable; respects reduced motion and festival days. Decide: fixed hours, or the real
  sunrise and sunset from the visitor's time zone (no location asked). About half a day.
- **Chant mode on every mantra page.** "Chant" turns the screen into a practice instrument: only the text, large, in
  its script; tap anywhere to count, a soft buzz each time and a stronger one at 108; the screen stays awake; the
  count goes into the practice record. About a day; most parts exist in the guided practices.
- **Welcome back.** For a returning visitor the homepage's first line becomes their own: "Good evening. Day 4 of your
  practice. Tonight: Saturday, with Shani." Worked out from the record in their browser; new visitors see the
  opening as it is. About half a day; shares groundwork with the time of day.

## From the earlier list (practice found more)

- **A picture for each mantra when its link is shared, and a Share button** on every mantra and practice page, so
  the library becomes a way in from search and messages.
- **The sacred days lead to that night's practice** (the Amavasya countdown and festival days), and reminders and
  "add to home screen" offered right after a first practice, not only on the tools page.

## Steer clear of

- Pop-ups and "before you go" offers: they undercut a sacred space.
- Streaks that punish a missed day, badges and scores: practice without guilt. "Day 4" is a gentle count only.
- A computer voice reading mantras: it would mispronounce sacred sounds. Real recordings later, as planned.

## Suggested order

1. Quick wins (about a day): Today's line learns your tradition, "Tomorrow", care at night, the still switch.
2. The signature (two to three days): time of day with welcome back, chant mode, share as picture.
3. Once visitors arrive: together today, pick up where you left off.
